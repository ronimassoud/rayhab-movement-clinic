# Asset register

Who made each image, font and third-party script on the site, and on what
terms it may be used. Fill in every **[DECIDE]** before launch: a
commissioned photographer or a stock library can hold rights the clinic has
not licensed, and a person who can be recognized in a photo should have
agreed to it being published.

Keep the signed documents themselves (licenses, model releases, patient
consent forms) with the clinic's records, not in this repository: it is
public, and some name patients. Record here only where each one is kept.

Sizes of one image (`-480`, `-960`, `-1600` and the `.jpg` fallback) count
as one asset.
