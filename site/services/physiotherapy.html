The approach

Treated actively, not passively

Passive treatment can feel good and rarely changes much on its own. What reliably changes a musculoskeletal problem is loading the tissue properly, restoring capacity, and understanding what provokes it. That is where the time goes here.

Loaded, not just rested

Tissue adapts to load. The question is the dose, not whether to load at all.

Provocation understood

What makes it worse, what settles it, and what it means, explained rather than worked around.

Discharged with a plan

You leave knowing what to do if it returns, which it sometimes does.

The rehabilitation process

Six stages, one direction

Not every problem needs all six. Simple problems move quickly, and being told that is part of an honest assessment.

  1. 01

    01

    Assessment

    History, examination, and what specifically you cannot currently do.

  2. 02

    02

    Explanation

    What the problem is and is not, in terms you can repeat to someone else.

  3. 03

    03

    Symptom control

    Settling irritability enough that useful loading becomes possible.

  4. 04

    04

    Loading

    Progressive exercise at a dose the tissue tolerates and benefits from.

  5. 05

    05

    Capacity

    Building past where you were, so the same demand no longer provokes it.

  6. 06

    06

    Discharge with a plan

    What to keep doing, what to watch for, and what to do if it returns.

Who this is for

Neck, back and shoulder complaints

Including the ones that have been present for a long time and have been managed passively so far.

Hip and knee rehabilitation

Post-injury and post-operative conditioning, and the long-standing niggles that limit training.

People who want to understand it

Where the goal is knowing what is happening and what to do, not open-ended appointments.

What the programme includes

  • Full musculoskeletal assessment
  • Explanation of findings in plain terms
  • Manual and symptom-control techniques where useful
  • Progressive exercise programme
  • Strength and capacity restoration
  • Home programme you can actually run
  • Discharge plan and review point

Rehabilitation outcomes vary between individuals. Rayhab does not promise a specific result or timeline.
