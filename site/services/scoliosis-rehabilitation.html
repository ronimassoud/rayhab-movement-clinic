The approach

A curve is not a line on an X-ray

Scoliosis involves rotation, alignment, posture, breathing mechanics and individual movement patterns at the same time. Rehabilitation here works on all of them, in the order that makes sense for your spine.

Three-dimensional correction

Elongation, de-rotation and stabilization worked together rather than in isolation.

Rotational breathing

Directed breathing strategies that reach the concave side of the curve.

Carried into daily life

Corrections rehearsed in sitting, walking, lifting and sport, not only on a mat.

Who this is for

Adolescents in a growth phase

Where monitoring and consistent corrective exercise matter most. Parents are part of every explanation.

Adults managing a known curve

Strength, mobility, pain management and a plan you can keep running for years.

Athletes training with scoliosis

Loading, rotation and sport demands built into the program rather than avoided.

What the program includes

  • Comprehensive scoliosis assessment
  • Individualized Schroth-based rehabilitation
  • Postural training and movement integration
  • Strength and conditioning where appropriate
  • Individualized home exercise program
  • Progress monitoring and review points
  • Patient and family education

Rehabilitation outcomes vary between individuals. Rayhab does not promise structural correction or guaranteed results.
