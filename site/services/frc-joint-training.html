The approach

Range you cannot control is not mobility

Most people can be stretched into more range than they can produce themselves. That difference is where injuries happen, because the joint has positions it can reach and cannot support. The work here is closing that gap.

Active before passive

Range you can produce under your own control, before range someone else puts you into.

End-range strength

Strength trained where joints are least protected, rather than only in the comfortable middle.

Variability

More than one way to perform a movement, so a single stiff joint does not dictate everything.

The rehabilitation process

Six stages, one direction

This is slow, unglamorous work that compounds. The stages describe the order, not a fixed number of weeks.

  1. 01

    01

    Assessment

    Joint-by-joint screen, with active and passive range recorded separately.

  2. 02

    02

    Explanation

    Where the gaps are, which ones matter for you, and which are simply variation.

  3. 03

    03

    Controlled articular work

    Rotational joint control drilled deliberately, one joint at a time.

  4. 04

    04

    End-range strength

    Loading the positions the joint could previously reach but not support.

  5. 05

    05

    Capacity

    Building tolerance so the new range survives training, work and fatigue.

  6. 06

    06

    Independent maintenance

    A short daily routine you run yourself, with periodic review.

Who this is for

Lifters and athletes

Where a position is limiting a lift or a skill, and stretching has stopped producing change.

People who feel stiff and stuck

Where range has slowly narrowed over years and general stretching has not held.

People managing a joint long term

After injury, or alongside a condition, where the goal is capacity rather than a cure.

What the programme includes

  • Joint-by-joint range assessment
  • Active and passive range recorded separately
  • Controlled articular rotation programme
  • End-range strength and capacity work
  • Integration into existing training
  • Short daily maintenance routine
  • Periodic re-measurement

Rehabilitation outcomes vary between individuals. Rayhab does not promise a specific result or timeline.
