Primary specialisation

Scoliosis Rehabilitation

Three-dimensional, Schroth-based rehabilitation for adolescents and adults. Assessment, individualised corrective exercise, rotational breathing, postural training, progression and long-term self-management.

Assessment, Schroth-based corrective exercise, breathing, progression and long-term self-management.

  • Comprehensive scoliosis assessment
  • Individualised Schroth-based programme
  • Breathing strategies and postural training
  • Home exercise programme and reviews
  • Strength and conditioning where appropriate
  • Online consultations and follow-ups
