The approach

Return is a decision, not a date

Most re-injury happens when someone returns because enough weeks have passed rather than because they can handle what the sport asks. The work here is building that capacity back deliberately, then testing it before it is trusted.

Criteria at every stage

Each progression has something you have to demonstrate before it is granted.

Loading rebuilt deliberately

Tissue capacity is regained in steps, with the dose written down rather than guessed.

The sport's real demands

Cutting, landing, throwing or sprinting rehearsed as they actually occur, not approximated.

The rehabilitation process

Six stages, one direction

How long each stage takes depends on the injury, the sport and the person. The order does not change.

  1. 01

    01

    Assessment

    Injury, history, loading tolerance and the demands of your sport.

  2. 02

    02

    Explanation

    What the problem is, what is irritable, and what the realistic timeline looks like.

  3. 03

    03

    Early loading

    Restoring range and beginning to load the tissue at a dose it can handle.

  4. 04

    04

    Strength restoration

    Rebuilding strength and capacity, including the side that was not injured.

  5. 05

    05

    Sport-specific

    Speed, change of direction, landing and contact reintroduced under supervision.

  6. 06

    06

    Return and prevention

    Cleared against criteria, with the work that keeps it from recurring.

Who this is for

Athletes returning from injury

Structured progression with the demands of your sport built into it, rather than general exercise until it feels better.

Recreational and weekend players

The same reasoning applied to a body that trains around a job, without pretending the schedule is professional.

Young athletes in a growth phase

Loading managed around growth, with parents and coaches part of the conversation.

What the programme includes

  • Injury and loading assessment
  • Sport-specific movement screen
  • Progressive loading programme
  • Strength and capacity restoration
  • Return-to-sport criteria testing
  • Home and gym programme
  • Injury prevention and movement preparation

Rehabilitation outcomes vary between individuals. Rayhab does not promise a specific result or timeline.
