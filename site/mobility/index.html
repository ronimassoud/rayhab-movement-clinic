Mobility & joint health

Flexibility is how far a joint can be moved. Mobility is how far you can move it yourself, with control, under load. The gap between those two is where most joint problems live.

A joint you can push into a position but cannot get into on your own has range you do not own. Closing that gap is the whole point of mobility training, and it is why stretching alone so often stops producing results.
