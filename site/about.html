The reason

Why Rayhab was created

Scoliosis care in the region too often stops at observation. A family leaves with an X-ray, a number, and an instruction to come back in six months, and no explanation of what the number describes or what could be done in between.

Rayhab was built around the alternative: a plan you understand and can carry out. Assessment that looks at the whole three-dimensional problem, exercise selected for your specific curve pattern, and measurement at review points so progress is visible rather than assumed.

One specialisation

Scoliosis rehabilitation is the centre of the practice, not one service among ten.

Built to hand over

The aim is a programme you run yourself, with periodic reviews, not indefinite appointments.

Honest about limits

No promises of structural correction, and no fear used as a reason to start treatment.

The approach

Four things held together

Rayan's practice sits where clinical physiotherapy, movement science and active rehabilitation meet, with scoliosis as the specialisation that pulls them together.

Clinical expertise

A physiotherapy foundation applied to musculoskeletal problems, not a training qualification stretched to fit them.

Movement science

Functional Range Conditioning and exercise therapy inform how load, control and joint capacity are progressed.

Active rehabilitation

You do the work, coached. Passive treatment alone does not change how a spine or a joint loads.

Specialised scoliosis care

Schroth-based, three-dimensional work that treats rotation, breathing and posture together.
