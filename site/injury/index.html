Injury rehabilitation

These six articles cover the problems people arrive with most often, and the one question everybody asks in the first session: how long is this going to take.

Most people who come to the clinic with a persistent problem have already tried rest, and some medication, and possibly a scan. The reason none of it worked is usually that none of it changed what the tissue can tolerate.
