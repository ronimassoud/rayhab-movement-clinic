Booking terms

Booking, payment and cancellation

How a request becomes an appointment, when and how you pay, and what happens if either of us needs to change or cancel.

Last updatedDraft of October 20, 2026
Applies toAppointments requested on this website
QuestionsWhatsApp +961 71 383 315
Draft for the clinic and its legal adviser, not yet in force. This page is not linked from the site and is hidden from search engines. Highlighted passages need a decision. Everything else describes how the booking system works as built on October 20, 2026.

In short

  • Booking online is a request. Nothing is reserved for good until the clinic confirms it.
  • Nothing is charged when you request.
  • Once your request is approved, you choose how to pay: at the clinic, or online where offered.
  • To change or cancel, message us on WhatsApp. [DECIDE] How much notice, and whether any fee applies.

Requesting an appointment

  • You can request a time from tomorrow up to 90 days ahead, at the clinic or online.
  • A request is not yet an appointment. The clinic replies on WhatsApp, approving it, proposing another time, or explaining why it can't take it.
  • We aim to reply within one working day. [DECIDE] The working days and hours this means.
  • While we review your request, the time you asked for is held for you for 24 hours. If we haven't replied by then, the request lapses and the time is released; you are welcome to request again. [DECIDE] A 24-hour hold can run out before "one working day" over a weekend or holiday. Either make the hold longer (a setting), or reply within 24 hours every day.

If we propose another time

If the time you asked for isn't possible, we may offer another. You get a private link on WhatsApp to accept it or ask for a different one. The new time is held for 24 hours; if it isn't accepted by then, it is released.

Prices and payment

  • Nothing is charged when you request.
  • Once your request is approved, we send a private link on WhatsApp. It shows the appointment and its price, and lets you choose how to pay. [DECIDE] Whether prices should also be shown before you request, and in which currency (US dollars, Lebanese pounds, or both).
  • Pay at the clinic: choosing this confirms your appointment, and you pay at reception when you come in.
  • Pay online with Whish, where offered: you pay on Whish's own page, and your appointment is confirmed once Whish confirms the payment. Coming back from Whish's page is not proof of payment on its own. [DECIDE] When online payment is offered; until then, payment is at the clinic only.
  • Please choose within 24 hours of approval; after that the clinic may offer the time to someone else.
  • The link is private to you. It works for 14 days, and never later than the day after the appointment.

Reminders

We send a reminder on WhatsApp about a day before a confirmed appointment, with the time and where it takes place.

Changing or cancelling

If you need to change or cancel

Message us on WhatsApp at +961 71 383 315, or reply to any of our messages, and we'll find another time or cancel it. [DECIDE] How much notice is needed, and whether a late cancellation carries a fee.

If we need to change or cancel

If the clinic has to cancel, we tell you on WhatsApp as early as we can and offer another time. You won't be charged for an appointment we cancel. [DECIDE] What happens to an online payment we have already received: a refund, or credit toward another appointment.

Missed appointments

If you can't make it, please tell us as early as you can, so someone else can have the time. [DECIDE] Whether a missed appointment without notice is charged, and how.

Online consultations

Online consultations take place by video call and are booked the same way. [DECIDE] Which video platform, how the link is sent, and whether anything differs for patients outside Lebanon.

Appointments for children

A parent or guardian can request an appointment for a child. [DECIDE] [LEGAL] Who must attend with the child, and who agrees to the assessment and treatment.
