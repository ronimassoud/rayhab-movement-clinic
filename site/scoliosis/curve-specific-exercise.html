Pillar 02 · The Schroth Method

Why the exercises have to be curve-specific

This is the single most important thing separating scoliosis-specific rehabilitation from general back exercise.

Reading time2 min read
Written byDr. Rayan Halimeh, Certified Schroth Practitioner
StatusAwaiting clinical review

In short

  • Corrections are directional, and the right direction depends on your curve pattern.
  • A correction that helps a right thoracic curve may do nothing for a left lumbar curve.
  • Symmetrical exercise treats an asymmetrical problem symmetrically.
  • This is why an assessment must establish the pattern before a program exists.

An asymmetrical problem

Scoliosis is asymmetrical by definition. One side is lengthened and one shortened; one side of the ribcage is collapsed and the other splayed. A program that does the same thing on both sides preserves the difference between them, because it adds equally to both.

That is not an argument that symmetrical exercise is harmful. It is an argument that it is not, by itself, scoliosis treatment.

Corrections have a direction

Every corrective position in a curve-specific program has a direction attached: lengthen here, turn this way, expand into that region, press down through that side. Reverse the pattern and you reverse the instructions.

Applying the corrections for a right thoracic curve to someone with a left thoracolumbar curve is not a smaller version of the right treatment. It is a different treatment, applied to the wrong person.

So is general exercise pointless?

No, and this is worth being clear about. General strength, mobility and cardiovascular fitness are valuable for anyone with a spine, including a curved one. They support the specific work and they matter for health in their own right.

What they are not is a substitute for the corrective component. The reasonable position is both, with the general work built so it does not fight the specific work.

The practical consequence

A scoliosis program cannot responsibly be handed out before someone has established the curve pattern, its direction, its rotation and how the trunk behaves under load. That is what an assessment is for, and it is why programs downloaded from the internet are a gamble rather than a plan.
