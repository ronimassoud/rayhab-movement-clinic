Patient experiences

What people say afterwards

Individual results vary. Rayhab does not promise structural correction or guaranteed outcomes.

What counts as progress

Measured, not remembered

Posture, rotation and function are re-measured at review points, so a change is something you can see in the record rather than something you have to take on trust. These are the things a journey is tracked against.

Functional improvement

What you can do, hold and load that you could not hold at the first assessment.

Movement progress

Control through range, breathing pattern, and how corrections carry into daily life.

Independence

Whether the programme has been handed over, run by you, with periodic review.

Privacy and consent

Nothing publishes without permission

Case studies, clinical images and quotes are used only with the patient's explicit consent, and that consent can be withdrawn.

The slow ones are published too

A record that only shows fast progress is not a record. Timelines are reported as they happened.

No outcome is promised

Rehabilitation outcomes vary between individuals. Nothing here should be read as a prediction of your own result.
