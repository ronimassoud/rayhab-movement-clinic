Privacy

How Rayhab looks after your information

What the clinic collects when you request an appointment or ask a question, why, who helps us handle it, how long we keep it, and how to get a copy or have it deleted.

Last updatedDraft of October 20, 2026
Applies toThis website, bookings and messages
Questionsrayan.halimeh@gmail.com
Draft for legal review, not yet in force. This page is not linked from the site and is hidden from search engines. Highlighted passages need a decision by the clinic or its legal adviser. Everything else describes the booking system as built on October 20, 2026.

In short

  • We collect what we need to arrange your appointment or answer your question, and nothing more.
  • This site uses no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.
  • Your details are kept by the clinic and a few services that run bookings and messages. They are never sold or used for marketing.
  • Anything you tell us about your health is seen only by the practitioners.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information, or for it to be deleted, at any time.

Who we are

Rayhab Movement Clinic, run by Dr. Rayan Halimeh, physiotherapist, in Beirut and Aley, Lebanon, decides how your information is used and is responsible for it. [DECIDE] The clinic's legal name, registration number and postal address.

For anything about your information, email rayan.halimeh@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +961 71 383 315. [DECIDE] A clinic address on dr-rayhab.com for privacy questions, once one exists.

What we collect

When you request an appointment

  • The service you choose, whether at the clinic or online, and the day and time you ask for.
  • Whether the appointment is for you or your child.
  • Your first and last name, your WhatsApp number, and your email address if you give one.
  • Anything you write under "What brings you in?". This is optional and may include health information, such as a diagnosis or how you feel. Please write only what helps us prepare.
  • That you agreed to the statements on the form, when, and which version of their wording you saw.

When you send a question

Your name, your email address, your phone number if you give one, and your question.

When you choose how to pay

The payment method, the amount and a payment reference. If you pay online with Whish, you do it on Whish's own page: your card or wallet details go to Whish, never to us.

Messages we send you

WhatsApp messages about your request: that we received it, that it is approved (with a private link to choose how to pay), a new time if we need to propose one, a reminder the day before, a cancellation, or a payment receipt. We keep a record of each message and whether it was delivered. Our messages never name the service you booked, so they say nothing about your health.

If you reply, your message is kept with your booking and handled like a question sent through this site. Photos, voice notes and documents you send there are not kept. Reply STOP at any time and we won't message you on WhatsApp again; we will call you instead if we need to reach you.

When you visit this site

Like any website, our server records the address your device connects from (IP address), your browser type, the page requested and the time. We use these records only to keep the site secure and to fix problems. Private payment links are never written to them.

What we don't do

  • No cookies for visitors, and nothing stored in your browser.
  • No analytics, advertising or social media tracking.
  • Fonts and scripts come from our own server, so no other company learns which pages you read.
  • The WhatsApp, Instagram and TikTok links are plain links: nothing from those services loads until you click.

Case studies

Our Results page shows patients' stories and posture photographs only with their written permission [DECIDE] and a parent's or guardian's for anyone under 18. Anyone featured can withdraw that permission at any time, and we will take their case down.

How we protect it

  • The site only works over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Clinic staff sign in with a password and a code from an authenticator app.
  • Every change to a booking is recorded, and so is who opened which record.
  • Each person at the clinic sees only what their role needs.
  • Our error records leave out email addresses and phone numbers.
  • [DECIDE] Backups are encrypted. State this only once backup encryption is switched on.

No system is perfectly secure. If something goes wrong that affects your information, we will tell you [LEGAL] when and how the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete it. Requests that never became appointments, questions and message records are deleted completely. For appointments that took place or were paid, we keep the date, service and payment for our records, but remove your name, contact details and note. [LEGAL] Subject to any legal duty to keep clinical or financial records.
  • Withdraw an agreement you gave, for example to a case study, or stop WhatsApp messages by replying STOP (we will then arrange things by phone).
  • [LEGAL] Any other rights under Lebanese law, and under the GDPR if it applies, such as objecting to a use of your information.

How to ask: email rayan.halimeh@gmail.com or message +961 71 383 315 on WhatsApp. To protect you, we confirm every request from the phone number or email address the information is held under. There is no charge, and we aim to reply within [LEGAL] 30 days.
