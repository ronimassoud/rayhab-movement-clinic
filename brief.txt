
Rayhab Movement Clinic
Master Brand, Website & Digital Project Guidelines
1. Project Overview
Rayhab Movement Clinic is a specialized physiotherapy and movement rehabilitation brand founded by Rayan Halimeh, a physiotherapist specializing primarily in scoliosis rehabilitation, with additional expertise in sports physiotherapy, movement rehabilitation, exercise therapy, mobility, and joint training.
The central objective of this project is to build Rayhab into a recognizable, trusted, premium scoliosis rehabilitation brand, beginning in Lebanon and designed from the outset to grow regionally and internationally.
Rayhab should not be positioned as another general physiotherapy clinic.
The brand should occupy a clear space in people's minds:
Rayhab = Specialized Scoliosis Rehabilitation
Everything developed for the brand, website, content, visual identity, messaging, and future digital products should reinforce this positioning.

4. Scoliosis Philosophy
Rayhab approaches scoliosis as a three-dimensional spinal condition involving rotation, alignment, posture, breathing mechanics, and individual movement patterns, rather than simply treating scoliosis as a lateral spinal curve.
The scoliosis philosophy should emphasize:
Individualized assessment
Three-dimensional correction
Schroth-based principles
Patient-specific exercise
Active rehabilitation
Breathing strategies
Postural awareness
Movement integration
Progressive exercise
Education and self-management
Long-term physical confidence
Communication should be scientifically grounded but understandable to patients and families.
Avoid exaggerated claims, fear-based messaging, promises to "fix" scoliosis, or language implying guaranteed structural correction.
The tone should be:
Reassuring, knowledgeable, modern, precise, empowering, and evidence-informed.

12. Target Audiences
The brand and website should communicate effectively with several audiences while preserving one unified identity.
Primary audiences may include:
Parents of adolescents with scoliosis
Often seeking clarity, reassurance, expertise, and a structured treatment plan.
Adolescents with scoliosis
Need communication that feels empowering rather than frightening or clinical.
Adults with scoliosis
May be looking for better movement, strength, exercise guidance, pain management, or long-term management strategies.
Athletes
Seeking rehabilitation, return-to-sport support, movement quality, and performance-oriented recovery.
General physiotherapy patients
Seeking active, exercise-based rehabilitation.
International / Online Clients
A future audience seeking specialized scoliosis consultations, education, and digital programs.

13. Content & Messaging Principles
All communication should prioritize:
Clarity over cleverness.
Expertise over hype.
Education over fear.
Specificity over generic wellness language.
Active rehabilitation over passive treatment.
Content should make complicated rehabilitation concepts understandable without oversimplifying them.
Avoid excessive technical jargon unless it is immediately explained.
Avoid language such as:
Miracle treatment
Fix your spine
Cure scoliosis
Perfect posture
Guaranteed correction
Permanent pain elimination
One exercise that solves scoliosis
Do not use fear to encourage treatment.

19. Geographic Strategy
Rayhab begins with a strong physical and professional presence in Lebanon.
The brand should nevertheless look internationally credible from its earliest stage.
Avoid making the identity so geographically specific that it becomes difficult to expand outside Lebanon.
The strategic progression should be:
Lebanon → Regional Recognition → Digital Reach → International Brand

20. Decision-Making Hierarchy
Whenever making a branding, design, copywriting, content, or website decision, evaluate it using the following order:
1. Does this strengthen Rayhab's association with scoliosis rehabilitation?
2. Does this increase credibility and trust?
3. Does this help patients understand the approach?
4. Does this distinguish Rayhab from generic physiotherapy clinics?
5. Does this support the broader movement and rehabilitation philosophy?
6. Can this scale as Rayhab grows?
If a creative idea looks attractive but weakens Rayhab's positioning, clarity, credibility, or scalability, it should not be prioritized.

21. Project Non-Negotiables
Throughout the project:
Scoliosis remains the dominant specialization.
Rayhab must not feel like a generic physiotherapy practice.
Branding should extend beyond a logo into a complete identity system.
The website should educate as well as convert.
Clinical claims must remain responsible and credible.
The brand must feel professional enough for healthcare and dynamic enough for movement.
The founder's expertise should support the brand without making the entire identity dependent solely on a personal brand.
The website must be built with future digital expansion in mind.
Content should be structured for long-term growth.
User experience should remain simple despite the depth of available information.
Evidence-based thinking should guide clinical messaging.
Every major decision should strengthen long-term brand equity rather than simply solving an immediate design need.
